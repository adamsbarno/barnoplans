About Barno Plans

Thoughtful plans for the way you want to live.

Barno Plans provides practical, modern architectural house plans for homeowners, families, and property developers. We create clear designs that balance beauty, comfort, and efficient use of space, from the first idea to a plan ready for professional review and construction.

Based in Nairobi and Eldoret, Kenya, we serve homeowners, families, and property developers worldwide. Our services include house-plan design, floor plans, elevations, building sections, and custom design adjustments.

Before you choose

Common questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before reviewing or purchasing a digital plan package.

What do I receive after payment?

You receive the PDF plan package listed on the selected plan page. Review the package details before purchasing.

Can a plan be changed?

Ask us about adjustments before purchasing. A qualified professional should review any changes for your site and local requirements.

Are the plans ready for construction?

Plans are design packages and may need local approval, structural work, and site-specific adaptation before construction.

How do I ask a question?

Contact Barno Plans by email, phone, or WhatsApp and include the plan name you are asking about.
